## Forks

A fork of this repository must, before it is distributed or deployed:

1. use its own name, domain and logo (see the trademark policy below);
2. remove or replace the frame and placard design and any Degent artwork with its own;
3. not present its output as a Degent, as part of the Decentralized Gentlemen Club collection, or as endorsed by us.

## Trademark policy

Our names and logos are covered by the policy in [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md). In short: **using the names to
refer to the project is fine** ("a fork of DegentClub/degent", "compatible with the degent.club mint"); **shipping
clones, collections or services under our marks is not.**
