# What a club update looks like

Club updates collect news for members: new mints, holder perks in [the Club](/club), and stories from the
community. Links to the chain use ordinals.com, for example
[an inscription page](https://ordinals.com/inscription/add1a568533d555dc3ac0a6db5b1ffa5d0f87d36b9b9059e4a8ce6bd7a94bfd3i0).

1. Mint in the [Atelier](/atelier) or bring your own art.
2. Follow the order on the mint page until it is delivered.
3. Sign in to the Club to see your Degents.
