# Why size matters on Bitcoin

A **Large Degent** (400 KB to 3.5 MB) or a **Full Block Degent** (3.5 to 3.9 MB) takes real block space. The
[Mint Process](/how-it-works) page explains the tiers, the lanes and what happens after you pay.

- Standard Degents share blocks with everyone else.
- Large Degents share a block with other Large Degents.
- A Full Block Degent fills a block on its own.
